Privacy Policy for WonderKids World

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • App Package: com.aistudio.wonderkids.wkzmpq

1. Overview

WonderKids World ("the App") is an offline-first educational gaming application designed for children aged 3–12 and their parents or guardians. Protecting children's privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information is (and is not) handled when you use the App.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

WonderKids World does not require user registration, login, or an online account, and we do not operate a backend server to collect personal data. Specifically, the App does not collect, store on external servers, or share:

  • Real names, email addresses, phone numbers, or home addresses
  • Precise or approximate device location
  • Photos, videos, contacts, or microphone recordings
  • User-generated content transmitted off the device

3. Local On-Device Storage

All learning progress and app preferences are stored strictly locally on your device using Android Room and Jetpack DataStore:

  • Optional child display nickname, chosen cartoon avatar, and selected age difficulty group (3–5, 6–8, or 9–12)
  • Earned stars, unlocked achievement badges, daily quest streaks, and module mastery statistics
  • Sound effects, background music, haptic feedback, and Parent Gate toggle preferences
Parents and guardians can erase all local progress at any time from the protected Parent Dashboard → Reset Local Progress or by uninstalling the App.

4. Advertising & Child-Directed Settings (Google AdMob)

WonderKids World integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) solely to display child-safe, non-personalized rewarded video ads after levels when an internet connection is available.

  • Child-Directed Configuration: Every ad request is configured with AgeRestrictedTreatment.CHILD and restricted to G-rated content (MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G).
  • No Advertising ID or Behavioral Tracking: We explicitly remove com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and Android Privacy Sandbox AdServices permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) from the App manifest so no cross-app tracking, interest profiling, or personalized advertising takes place.
  • Google Mobile Ads may process limited technical information (such as IP address for contextual ad delivery and fraud prevention) in accordance with Google's child-directed policies. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Device Permissions

The App requests only minimal, non-intrusive network permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used only to load rewarded ads when online. All educational modules, sound effects, and background music run 100% offline.

6. Parental Controls

WonderKids World includes a Parent Math Gate before entering the Parent Dashboard so adults can review accuracy and progress reports, calibrate age difficulty, or reset local device data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if features change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
